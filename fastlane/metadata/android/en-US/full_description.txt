The MyRenault app only allows a single charging window, identical every day. Many off-peak electricity plans have several windows per day, or whole off-peak days.

MyAuto Pilot keeps your real off-peak calendar on the phone and sends the right charging window to the car shortly before each off-peak period.

<b>Features</b>
• Off-peak windows per weekday, and whole off-peak days.
• "Ready by" targets (e.g. 80 % on Monday at 7:30): recurring schedules and one-off targets. A window that is too short is extended just enough.
• Automatic sending of the window to the car, at the exact time, even when the app is closed.
• Safe mode (on by default): each send is offered as a notification and only happens after you confirm.
• Dashboard: battery, range, location, remote climate control and charging.

<b>Language</b>: the interface is in French only for now.

<b>Compatibility</b>
Recent Renault electric vehicles managed by the MyRenault app (Rafale, Renault 5 and 4 E-Tech, Alpine A290, Master E-Tech…). Other models are read-only. A MyRenault account is required.

<b>Privacy</b>
Your MyRenault credentials are never stored. Your settings stay on your phone: no MyAuto Pilot server, no analytics, no ads.
